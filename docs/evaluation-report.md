# Evaluation report

Generated by `scripts/train_trigger.py` on 2026-10-16 17:36. All ML numbers are **out-of-fold**: every period is scored by a model that never trained on it, and the calibration map and the threshold θ for a fold never saw that fold either.

**Question:** will the river cross its own warning level within the next 2 h? Each river station's own JPS thresholds give the labels.

- **Data:** 15 river stations and 21 rain stations within 25 km, Apr 2024 → Oct 2026, 1,042,322 quarter hours below warning level.
- **Validation:** 4 time-blocked folds (calendar quarters, round-robin). Events are scored per (station, quarter).
- **Scoring:** a crossing is detected if the signal fired 3 h to 15 min before it; a false alarm is an alarm with no crossing in the next 3 h; alarm precision is the share of alarms followed by a crossing.
- **Inland vs tidal** is data-driven: a station is tidal if the lag-25 (6.25 h) autocorrelation of its level is below −0.2. Tidal: `3014080_`, `PANDAMARAN`.

**Limits:**
- Thresholds, calibration and the gate were fitted on the same archive that is scored, but never on the fold being scored. One indirect link remains: θ for fold k is chosen on the other folds' probabilities, and those come from models that trained on fold k. It is the usual practice and weak here (θ is the same in every fold).
- The extra false alarms of the two-tier signal sit at a few stations (see the per-station table, ratio column). With per-street alerts, streets linked to those stations get the extra Watch alerts.
- Events are scored per quarter block, so an alarm and a crossing on opposite sides of a quarter boundary are not matched.
- The tide comes from a global model (Open-Meteo Marine), not JUPEM tables.
